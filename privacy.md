# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 23, 2026_

QuickBooks Receipt Matcher (the "App") is a private, internal bookkeeping tool operated by Hopkins Realty ("we", "us") solely for our own business. It is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks users.

## Information the App accesses

With our authorization through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 sign-in, the App accesses our own QuickBooks Online company, including:

- transactions (expenses, credit card charges and credits, deposits, transfers and bill payments),
- the chart of accounts and classes,
- company name and existing attachments.

The App also processes receipts, invoices and bills that we choose to upload to it.

## How the information is used

The information is used only to:

- match our receipts and documents to our QuickBooks transactions,
- attach those documents to the matching transactions, and
- set the category (account) and class on those transactions.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we do not sell or rent it.

## Where information is stored

- The App runs locally on our own computer. QuickBooks access tokens and App settings are stored in local files on that computer and are not transmitted anywhere except to Intuit when refreshing access.
- Transaction data is read from QuickBooks as needed and is not stored in any separate database.
- Uploaded documents are held in memory only long enough to process them and attach them to QuickBooks.

## Third-party services

- **Intuit QuickBooks Online**: the App reads and updates our own company data through Intuit's API.
- **Anthropic (Claude API)**: uploaded documents are sent to Anthropic's API to extract information such as vendor, amount and date, along with the list of our account and class names. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms and privacy policy and does not use API data to train its models by default.

No other third parties receive the data.

## Security

Access to QuickBooks uses OAuth 2.0; we never see or store QuickBooks passwords. All communication with Intuit and Anthropic uses HTTPS. Credentials are kept in local, non-shared configuration files.

## Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time using the App's **Disconnect** button, or from QuickBooks Online under **Settings → Apps → Connected apps**. On disconnect, the App revokes its tokens with Intuit and deletes them locally.
