# Support

QuickBooks Receipt Matcher is a private tool maintained by Hopkins Realty.

**Email:** chopkinsrealty@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

When reporting a problem, please include:

- what happened and what you expected,
- the approximate time it happened, and
- the log file from **Download logs** in the app (or its **Support** page). It lists recent errors and QuickBooks request IDs (`intuit_tid`) and never contains passwords, tokens or keys.
