# QuickBooks Receipt Matcher

**Attach receipts to QuickBooks Online transactions and complete their category and class, automatically.**

QuickBooks Receipt Matcher streamlines bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online for Hopkins Realty. It automates the most time-consuming part of reviewing bank and credit card transactions: finding each transaction's receipt, attaching it, and choosing the right category and class.

## How it works with QuickBooks

1. Bank and credit card transactions are posted in QuickBooks Online as usual.
2. The user uploads receipts, invoices and bills to the app.
3. The app reads each document (vendor, amount, date) and finds the matching QuickBooks expense, deposit or transfer.
4. Using the QuickBooks Online Accounting API, it:
   - **attaches the document** to the transaction,
   - **sets or corrects the category** (expense account), and
   - **assigns the class** based on the business's rules.
5. Transactions that don't need a receipt, such as credit card payments and transfers, are recognized automatically. Uncertain matches are shown to the user to confirm before anything is written to QuickBooks.

The result is complete, audit-ready QuickBooks records without opening each transaction by hand.
