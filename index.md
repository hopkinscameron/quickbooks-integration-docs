# QuickBooks Receipt Matcher

QuickBooks Receipt Matcher is a **private, internal tool** used only by Hopkins Realty to keep its own QuickBooks Online books up to date. It is not offered to, sold to, or available for use by anyone else.

It runs locally on the owner's computer and:

- reads receipts, invoices and bills provided by the owner,
- matches them to transactions already posted in the business's QuickBooks Online company,
- attaches the documents to those transactions, and
- sets the transaction's category and class.

The app is launched and used locally; there is nothing to sign up for on this site.
