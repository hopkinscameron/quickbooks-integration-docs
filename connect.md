# Connect to QuickBooks

QuickBooks Receipt Matcher runs locally on Hopkins Realty's computer.

To connect or reconnect QuickBooks:

1. Start the app on your computer (`npm run dev` in the project folder).
2. Open **[http://localhost:3000/api/qbo/connect](http://localhost:3000/api/qbo/connect)**, or open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) and click **Connect to QuickBooks**.
